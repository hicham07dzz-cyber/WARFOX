# WARFOX Visual Quality Target

## Non-negotiable direction
WARFOX is intended to become a polished, realistic-looking third-person Battle Royale for Android. Primitive cubes, spheres, cylinders, and flat placeholder shapes are permitted only for temporary greyboxing and must never be presented as the finished visual style.

## Visual target
- Aim for a visual quality range comparable to mobile Battle Royale games such as Free Fire, while keeping WARFOX's own identity and original designs.
- Use detailed human character models, believable proportions, expressive faces, complete outfits, armor, and properly rigged animations.
- Use authored weapon and vehicle models with recognizable silhouettes, materials, and surface detail.
- Build a large island from natural terrain, roads, vegetation, rocks, water, and architecturally varied buildings.
- Use coherent materials, baked/efficient lighting where appropriate, shadows, atmospheric effects, weather, and polished UI.
- Maintain a consistent black, orange, and metallic-gray WARFOX art direction with the fox emblem.

## Production rules
1. Greybox geometry is for layout and gameplay testing only.
2. Replace placeholders with suitable licensed or original production assets before calling an area visually complete.
3. Do not claim realistic graphics, finished models, animations, or effects until they exist in the project and have been inspected in-engine.
4. Prefer optimized mobile assets, level-of-detail, sensible texture sizes, occlusion/culling, and scalable quality settings.
5. Prioritize one polished playable vertical slice before expanding the full island and all game systems.

## Honest scope
A high-quality 3D Battle Royale is a substantial production. The repository currently contains an early Godot prototype and a map design document, not a finished game. This target is a quality requirement, not a claim that these assets or systems have already been implemented.
